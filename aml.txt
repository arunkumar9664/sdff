You are a Senior Full-Stack Engineer, UI/UX Designer, Next.js Architect and Technical SEO specialist.

Build a complete production-quality corporate financial-services website for:

https://www.digamberfinance.com/

Study the reference website to understand its information architecture, business domain, content hierarchy, loan/product presentation, company information, leadership presentation, customer stories, investor relations, careers, CSR/impact, customer awareness, grievance resources, policies, regulatory information and footer structure.

IMPORTANT:
